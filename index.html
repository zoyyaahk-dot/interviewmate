What is Interview Mate? +

Interview Mate is an interview preparation platform where you can practice aptitude, coding and HR interview rounds.

How does the subscription work? +

Choose a monthly plan and complete the subscription payment through Razorpay. Your Interview Mate features are then unlocked.

Can I practice multiple interview rounds? +

Yes. Depending on your subscription, you can access the available aptitude, coding and HR interview modules.

Can I track my performance? +

Yes. Interview Mate provides dashboard statistics and progress information for your preparation.
